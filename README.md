# Swirl & Pop: concept website

A mobile-first single-page site for **Swirl & Pop**, a fictional event treat-cart rental business in Trinidad (soft serve, slushy, snow cone, popcorn, cotton candy and hot dog machines).

Concept build by **Portside Digital**: websites that work. Swirl & Pop is not a real business.

**Look:** Shrikhand + Bricolage Grotesque + DM Mono; bubblegum pink, mint, butter and plum ink on cream, with chunky cartoon outlines and a striped scalloped awning motif.

**Scroll craft** (GSAP + ScrollTrigger + Lenis, switched off for people who prefer reduced motion):
- Hero: an illustrated SVG treat cart rolls in, its awning springs open, and popcorn pops out of the kettle on a canvas; tap the cart for a burst. Scrolling rolls the cart away with spinning wheels
- Velocity-reactive marquee that speeds up and skews as you scroll
- Menu: pinned horizontal scroll on desktop (native swipe-snap on mobile), with a Party Starter / Machine only toggle that tweens every price
- How it works: pinned section where a soft-serve swirl draws itself tier by tier as you scroll, ending in a sprinkle shower
- Party builder: occasion, guest slider, machines, date, area, attendant. A live receipt totals the estimate and composes a WhatsApp order
- Events: sticky stacking cards that shrink back as the next one lands
- Sprinkle cursor trail on desktop, a scroll-progress bar in sprinkle colours, and a CTA where the words pop in like kernels

**Mobile:** sticky bottom dock (call / build / WhatsApp), safe-area padding, 16px inputs, no tap highlight, hover styles gated to mouse.

Call, WhatsApp, shop and social actions show a demo message instead of contacting anyone.
