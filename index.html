    Rapid-fire FAQs

    Quick answers.

    What's in a Party Starter package?

    The machine, ingredients for up to 50 servings, and basic delivery. Need more? Extra servings start from $300 per 50.

    Can I rent just the machine?

    Yes. Machine-only rentals include basic setup. Delivery and ingredients are priced separately, from $395 for the hot dog roller.

    How big an event can you handle?

    Anything from 50 to 1000+ guests. For big events we build a custom package with several machines, bulk ingredients and extended setup support.

    How much is delivery?

    Between $150 and $800 depending on location. Tell us your venue in the builder and we'll confirm the exact price.

    Do you send someone to serve?

    On request. Our attendants are an optional add-on, so you can enjoy your own party.

    How far ahead should I book?

    As early as you can, especially from November to January. Weekends fill up fast.
